# How to Donate[¶](https://scs.owasp.org/donate/how_to_donate/#how-to-donate "Permanent link")

**1\. Make your Donation:**

![](https://scs.owasp.org/assets/donations/owasp_donation_form.png)

Click the button to make your donation directly in the official OWASP website:

![⚠](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/jdecked/twemoji@16.0.1/assets/svg/26a0.svg ":warning:") Fill in the form and be sure to **select the option** *"Publicly list me as a supporter of OWASP Smart Contract Security"*

[🤍 Make your Donation](https://owasp.org/donate/?reponame=owasp-scs&title=OWASP+Smart+Contract+Security)

**2\. Register your Donation Package (optional):**

If your donation is above USD 500 you may opt-in for a [Donation Package](https://scs.owasp.org/donate/packages/) by registering it. We will then, together with the OWASP Foundation, verify and process it.

[Register your Donation](https://github.com/OWASP/owasp-scs/issues/new?assignees=warlordsam07&labels=org%2Cdonation&template=make-donation.yml&title=%F0%9F%92%B2%F0%9F%8E%89+New+Donation)
