# S6. Cryptographic Practices[¶](https://scs.owasp.org/SCSVS/10-SCSVS-CRYPTO/#s6-cryptographic-practices "Permanent link")

## Control Objective[¶](https://scs.owasp.org/SCSVS/10-SCSVS-CRYPTO/#control-objective "Permanent link")

Establish secure cryptographic practices for managing keys, verifying signatures, and generating random numbers to protect the integrity and authenticity of transactions and data within smart contracts.
